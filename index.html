Annelise Gill-Wiehl

Assistant Professor of Environmental Engineering · University of Notre Dame

We study how household environmental technologies perform in the real world, and how to make sure they deliver the health and climate benefits they promise.

ETHICA · Evaluating Technologies for Health Impacts, Climate, and Affordability

Research

i

Emissions & exposures

Measuring emissions and what people breathe.

ii

Field studies

Randomized trials to test how to increase adoption and sustained use, and how that use shapes health and well-being.

iii

Modeling at scale

Analyzing what households can afford, modeling the health and climate benefits at scale, and planning the energy systems needed to get there.

The burdens these technologies aim to reduce fall hardest on women and marginalized communities. Ethica, Latin for ethics, reflects our commitment to ethical research that translates into tangible impact for them.

Questions We're Asking

  • How do households reach exclusive use of clean cooking fuels?Tanzania
  • What can households actually afford, and what does that mean for consistent use?Sub-Saharan Africa
  • What will it take for central and decentralized grids to support electric cooking?Global
  • How high are residential exposures, and how do we reduce them?Global
